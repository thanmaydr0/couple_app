## Switching themes on Starter

There is no eight-mode dropdown on this plan. Rebind matching semantic names from Color Midnight to the chosen Color <Theme> collection, including descendants' fills/strokes and gradient-stop aliases. Merely setting an explicit mode on another collection does not replace an existing binding. Preserve geometry/styles. Auto is Rose daytime; Midnight is night. A verified all-theme shared-component rebind contact sheet remains outstanding due to quota. Follow FIGMA_BRIEF.md.
